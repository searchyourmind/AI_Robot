## Evidence actually inspected

The source URLs and relevant extracted text were read. T1 pp3, 4 and 8 were rendered locally and visually inspected to resolve merged rating/truth-table cells and package orientation. This is **source-document inspection**, not electrical measurement or CAD inspection. The datasheet download was hashed. Local `pdftotext` was unavailable; browser extraction and PDF rendering supplied the relevant text/visual checks. No manufacturer PDF is redistributed in this repository.

## Remaining real-system / release inputs

Exact motor ratings and stall/startup behavior; source/battery voltage, protection and returned energy; actual harness and GPIO/provider behavior; chassis/enclosure and mounting data; manufacturer stackup, copper/plating tolerances, stencil/reflow and assembly service remain unresolved. In particular, the reference requires controlled charging/shutdown and is not approved for abrupt Pi-logic loss with a charged motor rail. No pricing, inventory, manufacturer acceptance, shipping commitment or booking has been verified.
